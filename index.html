Frequently asked questions

How fast can you deploy a driver?

Most requirements move from a shared brief to a first candidate interview within 48 hours. Bulk fleet requirements are scoped individually based on city and driver category.

What happens if a driver leaves mid-contract?

Under our standard SLA, we provide a replacement driver within 24 hours so your fleet isn't left short.

Which areas do you cover?

Delhi-Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad, and Ghaziabad today, with additional NCR coverage planned.

How are drivers verified?

Every driver clears police verification, license authentication, background and reference checks, a driving assessment, a fitness screening, and soft-skills orientation before being shortlisted.

Do you support both short-term and long-term hiring?

Yes — pay-per-hire suits one-off needs, while a monthly retainer or enterprise contract suits ongoing fleet staffing.

What vehicle categories do your drivers operate?

Our drivers are authenticated and trained for all Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) transport categories — including aggregator cabs, sedan fleets, corporate employee shuttle cabs, SUVs, luxury cars, and EV fleets across Delhi NCR.
